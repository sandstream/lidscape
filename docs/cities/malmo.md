# Malmö

Proportion ratios are visual estimates for drawing. Heights and years are sourced.

## Viewpoint

The pier of Ribersborgs kallbadhus (the cold-bath house on Ribersborg beach), looking east-north-east back at the city across the shallow bay.

- Left (north): Turning Torso in Västra hamnen, near and very tall.
- Middle: Malmö Live's hotel tower, with the old town behind it.
- Middle-right: Malmöhus castle, low, behind the trees of Slottsparken.
- Right, far: the tower of S:t Petri kyrka above the old town.
- Foreground: open water, then the long wooden pier and the bath house on stilts.
- Behind the viewer (south-west): Öresundsbron, not in the scene.

## Landmarks

### Turning Torso
- Height: sources differ. 190.4 m and 193 m (192.94 m in the infobox) both appear in the Swedish Wikipedia article. 54 storeys counting mezzanines.
- Completed: inaugurated 27 August 2005.
- Position: left, near.
- Silhouette: nine stacked segments, each five storeys with a pentagon plan, each turned a little more than the one below. The top segment is turned 90 degrees from the ground floor. A thin steel spine runs up one side. Width ≈ 1/7 of height. Flat top. In a flat outline, show the twist as small steps that shift left and right.
- Local cue: the twist. It is the world's first twisted skyscraper.

### Malmö Live (Clarion Hotel & Congress)
- Height: 85 m (25 storeys in the highest building).
- Completed: inaugurated 4 June 2015.
- Position: middle, mid-distance.
- Silhouette: cluster of stacked boxes of different heights; the tallest is a plain slab; the lower boxes are offset like a pile of blocks.
- Local cue: the stepped stack of glass boxes by the canal.

### Malmöhus slott
- Height: not found in sources.
- Year: the medieval castle is dated 1434 (infobox). Rebuilt under Christian III, usually given as 1537 to 1542.
- Position: middle-right, mid-distance, low.
- Silhouette: long low brick range with stepped gables; two big round cannon towers with low conical roofs at the corners; a square gate tower; moat in front.
- Local cue: the fat round brick towers.

### S:t Petri kyrka
- Height: tower 98 m (English Wikipedia, after Wahlöö 2014). Swedish sources found give no tower height.
- Year: church from the first half of the 1300s. The present tower was rebuilt after collapses in 1420 and 1442. The current spire is from 1890.
- Position: right, far.
- Silhouette: very long brick nave with steep roof; square west tower, width ≈ 1/7 of height; tall narrow copper spire with a gallery-like ring and small corner points; cross.
- Local cue: the green copper spire over the old town.

### Ribersborgs kallbadhus
- Height: not found in sources.
- Year: moved to Ribersborg in 1898. Rebuilt after the storm of Christmas 1902.
- Position: foreground, middle, on the water.
- Silhouette: low wooden pavilion on stilts with pitched roofs and small towers, at the end of a long pier.
- Local cue: the bath house standing in the sea.

## Filler

The old town (Gamla staden): low merchant houses and half-timbered (korsvirke) houses around Lilla torg, with steep red tile roofs and stepped brick gables. Colours are pale yellow, pink, white and red brick. Västra hamnen: new mid-rise white and grey flat-roofed blocks along the water.
