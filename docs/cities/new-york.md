# New York

Coordinates: 40.722° N, 73.996° W. Source: [Natural Earth populated places](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/downloads/10m-cultural-vectors/10m-populated-places/), New York point, version 5.1.2.

Proportion ratios are visual estimates for drawing. Heights and years are sourced. Feet are converted at 1 ft = 0.3048 m.

## Viewpoint

Gantry Plaza State Park, Long Island City (Queens), looking west across the East River at Midtown Manhattan.

- Left, far: One World Trade Center, small in the distance down the island.
- Left-middle: the Empire State Building.
- Middle: the Chrysler Building, with One Vanderbilt rising just behind it.
- Middle, near: the UN Secretariat, a thin slab on the riverbank.
- Right: 432 Park Avenue, a very thin square pencil tower.
- Foreground: the river, and the park's black steel railway gantries on the near quay. The Pepsi-Cola sign stands in the park, facing Manhattan.

## Landmarks

### Empire State Building
- Height: 1,454 ft (443.2 m) to the tip; roof 1,250 ft (381 m). 102 floors.
- Completed: 11 April 1931; opened 1 May 1931.
- Position: left-middle, far.
- Silhouette: broad base; a series of setbacks that step in; a tall slim shaft with vertical stripes; small setbacks near the top; a rounded mast with ribs; thin antenna.
- Local cue: the stepped shaft and the mast. It is the classic stepped Art Deco tower.

### Chrysler Building
- Height: 1,046 ft (318.8 m) to the spire; roof 925 ft; 77 floors.
- Completed: 27 May 1930.
- Position: middle, mid-distance.
- Silhouette: slim brick shaft; a crown of stacked, shrinking stainless steel arches, each with triangular windows in a sunburst pattern; a long needle spire.
- Local cue: the shiny arched crown. Cut the triangle windows as small lit holes.

### One Vanderbilt
- Height: 1,401 ft (427 m) with spire; roof 1,301 ft (396.5 m). 62 storeys.
- Opened: 14 September 2020.
- Position: middle, far, just behind and right of the Chrysler Building.
- Silhouette: four tapering glass faces that step in toward the top, like a stack of slimming prisms; the faces end at different heights, then a short spire.
- Local cue: the tapering glass crown with a pointed tip.

### United Nations Secretariat
- Height: 155.3 m. 39 floors.
- Completed: 9 October 1952.
- Position: middle, near, on the riverbank.
- Silhouette: a thin flat slab seen broad side on from this view; width about half its height; flat top. A low curved General Assembly building sits beside it.
- Local cue: the plain slab on the water.

### 432 Park Avenue
- Height: 1,396 ft (425.5 m). 85 floors.
- Completed: 23 December 2015.
- Position: right, far.
- Silhouette: a perfectly square, very thin column, width ≈ 1/15 of height; a regular grid of large square windows; open floors (dark bands) at intervals; flat top.
- Local cue: the extremely thin grid stick.

### One World Trade Center
- Height: 1,776 ft (541.3 m) architectural; 1,792 ft (546.2 m) to the tip; roof 1,368 ft (417 m).
- Opened: 3 November 2014.
- Position: far left, very far.
- Silhouette: square base; the edges taper as the tower rises, so the faces become triangles and the top is a smaller square turned 45 degrees; a tall thin spire.
- Local cue: the tapering glass tower with a spire, alone at the island's tip.

## Traits

- snow: true. Central Park gets about 75.7 cm (29.8 in) of snow a year, on
  about 11 days. Snow depth peaks in February at about 20 cm on average.
- adventStars: false. Lit paper stars in windows are a German and Swedish
  custom. No source found names it as a New York custom.
- ice: true. Ice floes filled the East River in February 2015, and a cargo ship
  moved slowly through the ice. The scene shows floes, not a frozen river.

## Rights

- Rule: 17 U.S.C. § 120(a). The copyright in a constructed architectural work
  does not include the right to prevent pictures of it, or their distribution
  and public display. This applies when the building is in, or ordinarily
  visible from, a public place. The section has no non-commercial limit.
- All six landmarks are buildings visible from public places. The drawn view
  may show them by day and at night.
- Trademarks are a separate matter. ESRT Empire State Building, L.L.C. holds
  registered trademarks in images of the building. It licenses the name and
  image for promotional and advertising use. Do not use the Empire State
  Building in the app icon or in marketing images without a check.
- The Pepsi-Cola sign is a trademark logo. It faces west toward Manhattan, so a
  viewer behind it sees its back. Leave out the Pepsi-Cola lettering.

## Filler

Midtown: a dense mass of flat-topped office slabs and wedding-cake setback towers, with water tanks on the roofs of lower buildings. Colours are grey stone, brown brick, and dark glass. Along the river: low warehouses and new glass apartment towers.
