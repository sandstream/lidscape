# Helsingfors (Helsinki)

Coordinates: 60.164° N, 24.932° E. Source: [Natural Earth populated places](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/downloads/10m-cultural-vectors/10m-populated-places/), Helsinki point, version 5.1.2.

Proportion ratios are visual estimates for drawing. Heights and years are sourced.

## Viewpoint

The water of Eteläsatama (the South Harbour), as seen from a ferry or the Katajanokka quay edge, looking north at Kauppatori (the Market Square).

- Left: the Old Market Hall on the quay, then the Presidential Palace behind the square.
- Middle: the City Hall's pale facade on the square. Behind and above it, the green dome of the Cathedral (Tuomiokirkko) on Senate Square.
- Right: Uspenski Cathedral, red brick with gold onion domes, on the Katajanokka rock. SkyWheel turns at the water's edge below it.
- Foreground: open harbour water, then the market quay with tents and boats.

## Landmarks

### Helsingin tuomiokirkko (Helsinki Cathedral)
- Height: 62 m (Swedish Wikipedia).
- Year: built 1830 to 1852. Inaugurated 15 February 1852.
- Position: middle, far, raised (it stands on a high plinth above Senate Square).
- Silhouette: white block with columned porticoes; tall drum; high green dome; small lantern; cross. Four small domed corner towers around the main dome. Twelve apostle statues stand on the roof edges.
- Local cue: the white church with one big and four small green domes.

### Uspenskin katedraali (Uspenski Cathedral)
- Height: not found in a reliable source. Travel sites say 33 m for the main dome; treat this as unconfirmed.
- Year: built 1862 to 1868. Inaugurated 25 October 1868. Domes regilded 2004 to 2007.
- Position: right, mid-distance, on a rock.
- Silhouette: red brick church on a granite cliff; steep green roofs; a central tower with a big gold onion dome and cross; smaller gold onion domes around it at lower levels, 13 in all by most counts; a separate belfry with its own onion dome.
- Local cue: gold onions on red brick, high on the rock.

### Presidentinlinna (Presidential Palace)
- Height: not found in sources.
- Year: completed 1820 as the Heidenstrauch merchant house. Bought in 1837 as the Russian emperors' palace in Finland.
- Position: left, mid-distance, on the square's edge.
- Silhouette: long three-storey neoclassical block; flat low roof; slightly raised centre with a pediment or attic. Pale plaster facade.
- Local cue: the long pale block facing the harbour.

### Helsingin kaupungintalo (City Hall)
- Height: not found in sources.
- Year: completed 1833. Designed by Carl Ludvig Engel.
- Position: middle, mid-distance, on Pohjoisesplanadi facing the market.
- Silhouette: plain neoclassical block; low pitched roof; a central bay with columns and a pediment-like top. Pale plaster facade.
- Local cue: the columned centre bay over the market square.

### Vanha kauppahalli (Old Market Hall)
- Height: not found in sources.
- Year: opened 1889. Designed by Gustaf Nyström.
- Position: left, near, on the quay.
- Silhouette: long low brick hall; steep roof; decorated gable ends.
- Local cue: the brick market hall on the water.

### SkyWheel Helsinki
- Height: 40 m. 30 cabins.
- Year: opened 2014.
- Position: right, near, at the water below Uspenski.
- Silhouette: a ring on two A-frame legs; spokes; small cabins round the rim.
- Local cue: the wheel in front of the gold domes.

## Traits

- snow: true. On 13 January 2021 the Finnish Meteorological Institute recorded
  15 cm of new snow in one day at Kaisaniemi in central Helsinki, and 36 cm at
  Helsinki-Vantaa airport.
- adventStars: true, with a weaker source than the other traits. Yle says lit
  window stars came from Germany, became popular in Sweden in the 1930s and
  came to Finland after the Second World War. Yle does not say how common they
  are in Finnish windows today.
- ice: true. The water of Eteläsatama was ice-covered in the winter of 2018. In
  2020 an old ship kept the harbour open by circling 400 times in 24 hours.

## Rights

- Freedom of panorama: buildings may be depicted freely, also for commercial
  use. The statute is tekijänoikeuslaki (404/1961), § 25 a: "Rakennuksen saa
  vapaasti kuvata."
- A work of art placed permanently in a public place may also be depicted. If
  the work is the main subject of the picture, the picture may not be used for
  commercial purposes.
- Copyright lasts 70 years after the year of the author's death. The building
  rule has no time limit, so the death year of an architect does not limit the
  drawing.
- Special protection: § 25 a names none. I found no rule on the lighting of
  these landmarks.

## Filler

The Empire-style centre: low neoclassical blocks with low hipped tin roofs, painted pale yellow, cream, light blue and white, with white trim. Katajanokka: Jugend (Art Nouveau) blocks in grey granite and plaster with towers and steep roofs.
