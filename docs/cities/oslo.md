# Oslo

Coordinates: 59.919° N, 10.748° E. Source: [Natural Earth populated places](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/downloads/10m-cultural-vectors/10m-populated-places/), Oslo point, version 5.1.2.

Proportion ratios are visual estimates for drawing. Heights and years are sourced.

## Viewpoint

The harbour basin south of Akershus, on the ferry route to Hovedøya, looking north at the waterfront.

- Left: Rådhuset's two brick towers above Pipervika, with Aker Brygge beside it.
- Middle, near: Akershus festning on its rock, walls down to the water.
- Middle, far: the green tower of Oslo domkirke above the centre.
- Right: the white sloping Opera in Bjørvika, with MUNCH's leaning-top tower behind it, and the Barcode row of slim blocks.
- Far left, on the ridge: Holmenkollbakken's ski jump against the sky.
- Foreground: open fjord water.

## Landmarks

### Oslo rådhus (City Hall)
- Height: two towers, 63 m and 66 m. The ground at the west tower is about 3 m higher, so both tops are at the same level.
- Year: built 1931 to 1950. Inaugurated 1950.
- Position: left, mid-distance.
- Silhouette: wide low brick centre block; two square towers, one on each side; flat roofs on the towers with a small clock or bell stage. The towers are plain, almost box-like, width ≈ 1/3 of height.
- Local cue: two identical red brick towers like bookends.

### Akershus festning (Akershus Fortress)
- Height: not found in sources.
- Year: the castle was likely finished around 1300. Renaissance rebuilding from the early 1600s (first stage done 1604).
- Position: middle, near.
- Silhouette: grey stone walls on a rock above the water; a renaissance castle with steep copper roofs; several towers, two of them stair towers (Blåtårnet and Romerikstårnet) with pointed caps.
- Local cue: the fortress walls and spiky green roofs right on the water.

### Oslo domkirke (Oslo Cathedral)
- Height: not found in sources. A watch room was built in the tower in 1850.
- Year: inaugurated 7 November 1697. Tower rebuilt 1848 to 1850 (Chateauneuf).
- Position: middle, far.
- Silhouette: square tower with clock faces; green copper top stage; slim spire. The neighbouring Bazaar building has a matching green copper tower.
- Local cue: the green spire in the city centre.

### Den Norske Opera & Ballett (Oslo Opera House)
- Height: 54 m (above the stage); 207 m long, 110 m wide.
- Year: opened 12 April 2008 (completed 2008).
- Position: right, near, at the water.
- Silhouette: a white marble roof that slopes from the water up to a flat top, like a glacier; a taller box (the stage tower) rises at the back.
- Local cue: the white sloping roof people walk on.

### MUNCH
- Height: sources differ. 57.4 m above the shore (Dezeen); 58 m (ArtDependence); a 60 m tower (Designboom). 13 storeys.
- Year: opened 22 October 2021.
- Position: right, mid-distance, behind the Opera.
- Silhouette: tall slab; the top storeys bend forward toward the fjord, like a head nodding. Clad in perforated aluminium panels.
- Local cue: the bent top.

### Holmenkollbakken
- Height: sources differ. The rebuilt jump (2008 to 2010) is given as about 58 m in the air, a start house about 65 m above ground (SNL), or a 69 m inclined cantilever (the architects). Top at about 418 m above sea level.
- Year: first opened 1892. Present structure rebuilt 2008 to 2010.
- Position: far left, far, on the ridge.
- Silhouette: thin steel in-run that rises at an angle, then juts out past its support like a diving board; a slim mast at the top.
- Local cue: the ski jump on the skyline.

## Traits

- snow: true. Blindern station data show snow on Christmas Eve in two of every
  three years since 1940.
- adventStars: true. Store norske leksikon names the advent star in the window
  as one of the most common Advent symbols.
- ice: true. Bjørvika froze over every winter until well past the middle of the
  1800s. In the cold winter of 2009/10 the fjord froze in many places, and ice
  and currents apparently pried granite blocks loose at the Opera. The scene
  shows floes along the quay, and wider cover only with a lot of snow.

## Rights

- Freedom of panorama: buildings may be depicted freely, also for commercial
  use. The statute is åndsverkloven (Lov om opphavsrett til åndsverk mv.,
  LOV-2018-06-15-40), § 31: "Byggverk kan fritt avbildes."
- A work of art placed permanently in a public place may also be depicted. This
  does not apply when the work is clearly the main motif and the use is
  commercial.
- Copyright lasts 70 years after the author's death (§ 11). § 31 frees
  buildings, so the death year of an architect does not limit the drawing. This
  also covers the recent buildings in the view: the Opera, MUNCH and the
  rebuilt Holmenkollbakken.
- Special protection: § 31 names none. I found no rule on the lighting of these
  landmarks.

## Filler

The centre behind the harbour: mid-rise stone and plaster blocks from around 1900, with hipped roofs and some copper domes. Colours are ochre, cream, pale grey and brick red. Bjørvika: tall, thin modern blocks with gaps between them (the Barcode).
