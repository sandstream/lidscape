# Göteborg

Proportion ratios are visual estimates for drawing. Heights and years are sourced.

## Viewpoint

Ramberget (Keillers park) on Hisingen, looking south-south-east across Göta älv at the city centre.

- Left (east): Lilla Bommen's striped tower, Barken Viking at the quay, the Opera beside it.
- Middle-left, near: Karlatornet on the north bank at Lindholmen, far taller than anything else.
- Middle, far: Skansen Kronan on its hill above Haga.
- Middle-right: Masthuggskyrkan on Stigberget's rock.
- Far right (south-west): the two pylons of Älvsborgsbron.
- Foreground: the wide river, with ferries and cranes on the near bank.

## Landmarks

### Karlatornet
- Height: 246 m (73 storeys). One sentence in the same source says 245 m.
- Completed: exterior finished 2024. Tallest building in the Nordic countries.
- Position: middle-left, near (on the viewer's side of the river).
- Silhouette: very slim tower, width ≈ 1/8 of height; straight sides; the top steps in a little with a cut-off crown. Draw it as one tall pale-gold slab.
- Local cue: its height alone. It dwarfs everything in the view.

### Lilla Bommen ("Läppstiftet", the Lipstick)
- Height: 86 m (22 storeys). Completed: 1989. Architect Ralph Erskine.
- Position: left, mid-distance, at the water's edge.
- Silhouette: slim tower with red and white horizontal stripes; the top section is red and narrower, like a lipstick tip; short mast.
- Local cue: the red-and-white stripes.

### Barken Viking
- Height: mast height not found in sources. Length 108 m including bowsprit.
- Launched: 1 December 1906. Moored at Lilla Bommen since 1951.
- Position: left, near, in front of Lilla Bommen.
- Silhouette: white hull; four tall masts with yards (crossbars); thin rigging lines. The masts are taller than the 45 m clearance of Älvsborgsbron.
- Local cue: four masts beside the striped tower.

### GöteborgsOperan
- Height: 32 m. Length 160 m.
- Completed: October 1994 (infobox: 30 September 1994).
- Position: left, near, right of Barken Viking.
- Silhouette: long low building with roofs that slope up toward the river like a ship's bow; glass front; dark red cladding.
- Local cue: the sloping "prow" over the water.

### Skansen Kronan
- Height: 33 m (stands 87 m above sea level).
- Built: 1687 to 1689 (infobox gives 1689 to 1700).
- Position: middle, far, on a hill.
- Silhouette: rocky hill; squat six-sided granite tower, width more than half its height; four storeys of small gun ports; pointed roof; gilded crown on the peak (one travel source says a gilded cross; check a photo).
- Local cue: the crown on the hilltop fort.

### Masthuggskyrkan
- Height: tower 60 m, with 11 m sides. The rooster on top is 127 m above sea level.
- Inaugurated: 1914.
- Position: middle-right, mid-distance, on a rock.
- Silhouette: red brick church on a granite cliff; square tower, width ≈ 1/5.5 of height (11 m by 60 m); steep pyramid roof; short spire with a 1.5 m ball, cross and rooster.
- Local cue: the high brick tower on the rock, visible from ships entering the harbour.

### Älvsborgsbron
- Height: 107.6 m (pylons). Clearance 45 m. Main span 417.6 m.
- Inaugurated: 8 November 1966. Infobox gives completion as December 1967.
- Position: far right, far.
- Silhouette: two slim portal pylons, each two legs with a crossbeam; long flat deck; two hanging main cables in shallow curves.
- Local cue: the big suspension bridge at the harbour mouth.

## Filler

The south bank: mid-rise stone and plaster blocks from around 1900, with hipped roofs, small towers and copper domes on corner buildings. Colours are sandstone, ochre, brick red and grey. Majorna and Masthugget have wooden "landshövdingehus" (stone ground floor, wood above). The near bank on Hisingen is former shipyard land: cranes, warehouses and new glass blocks.
