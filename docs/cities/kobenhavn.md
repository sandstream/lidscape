# København

Proportion ratios are visual estimates for drawing. Heights and years are sourced.

## Viewpoint

The quay in front of Operaen on Holmen, looking west-south-west across Inderhavnen (the inner harbour).

- Left, near: Vor Frelsers Kirke's spiral spire on Christianshavn.
- Middle: Slotsholmen. Børsen's long roof by the water, Christiansborg's tower behind it, the Rådhus tower further back.
- Middle-right: Nikolaj's spire in the old city.
- Right: the great dome of Marmorkirken, with the low Amalienborg palaces on the waterfront in front of it.
- Foreground: the wide harbour, then the opposite quay with its warehouses.

## Landmarks

### Vor Frelsers Kirke (Church of Our Saviour)
- Height: sources differ. 90 m (Structurae), 95 m (Lonely Planet). The church's own site says the outer stair reaches 86 m.
- Year: church inaugurated 1695 (Wikipedia gives 1696 as completion). Spire by Lauritz de Thurah finished 1752.
- Position: left, near.
- Silhouette: square brick tower, width ≈ 1/8 of height; tall black spire made of an open spiral stair that winds round the outside; gilded handrail; gilded globe with a figure of Christ on top.
- Local cue: the corkscrew spire with the outside stair.

### Børsen (Old Stock Exchange)
- Height: spire 56 m. Building about 128 m long and 21 m wide.
- Year: built about 1619/1620 to 1623/1624. Spire 1625; replaced with a similar spire in 1775.
- Status: fire on 16 April 2024 toppled the spire. Reconstruction began 26 September 2024. Projected completion 2029. Draw the historic form.
- Position: middle, near, along the water.
- Silhouette: very long low brick building; a row of ornate Dutch gables on the roof; in the middle, a needle spire made of four dragon tails twisted together; three crowns on the spire.
- Local cue: the twisted dragon-tail spire.

### Christiansborg Slot
- Height: tower 106 m, the tallest tower in Copenhagen.
- Completed: 1928 (built 1907 to 1928). Two extra crowns added to the tower in 1934. Roof changed from black tile to copper in 1937.
- Position: middle, mid-distance, behind Børsen.
- Silhouette: massive granite palace block with a copper roof; central square tower; open lantern stage; tall pointed copper spire carrying crowns and a weather vane.
- Local cue: the heavy tower with crowns, above the long copper roof.

### Københavns Rådhus (City Hall)
- Height: tower 105.6 m.
- Completed: inaugurated 12 September 1905 (built 1892 to 1905).
- Position: middle, far.
- Silhouette: slim square brick clock tower, width ≈ 1/12 of height; clock faces; tall pointed copper spire.
- Local cue: the thinnest tall tower in the view, almost the same height as Christiansborg's.

### Sankt Nikolaj Kirke (Nikolaj Kunsthal)
- Height: the Danish Wikipedia article states a 90 m spire. The same article also says the tower is 35 m.
- Year: tower from 1591. Church burned in 1795. The present spire is a reconstruction, inaugurated 1909.
- Position: middle-right, mid-distance.
- Silhouette: square brick tower; a fanciful neo-baroque copper spire with several stacked bulbs and open stages; long needle.
- Local cue: the stacked-bulb green spire rising from a tower with no church.

### Marmorkirken (Frederik's Church)
- Height: 79 m (lex.dk, for the dome). Dome span 31 m, the largest church dome in Scandinavia.
- Year: inaugurated 1894, 145 years after the foundation stone.
- Position: right, mid-distance.
- Silhouette: round drum ringed with columns; high copper dome, steeper than a half sphere; ribbed; lantern; short gilded spire. Two small bell turrets flank the front.
- Local cue: the big green dome behind Amalienborg.

## Filler

Christianshavn and Nyhavn: low 18th century townhouses and warehouses, with steep red tile roofs, dormer windows and gable hoists. Colours are ochre, red, pale yellow, pink and blue plaster. Slotsholmen: long copper roofs, turned green. The harbour front has converted brick warehouses with black roofs.
