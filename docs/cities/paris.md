# Paris

Proportion ratios are visual estimates for drawing. Heights and years are sourced.

## Viewpoint

Pont de la Concorde, looking west-south-west down the Seine.

- Left, near: the colonnade of the Palais Bourbon (Assemblée nationale) at the south end of the bridge.
- Left, mid-distance: the gilded dome of the Invalides above the trees.
- Middle, far: the Eiffel Tower, standing alone over the river.
- Middle, near: Pont Alexandre-III crossing the river, with its four tall gilded pylons.
- Right: the long glass roof of the Grand Palais beyond the trees of the Cours la Reine.
- Foreground: the Seine with its stone quays and moored barges.

Not in this view: Notre-Dame (east, behind the viewer), Sacré-Cœur (north), Tour Montparnasse (south).

## Landmarks

### Tour Eiffel
- Height: 330 m with antennas (2022). 312 m when built.
- Built: 1887 to 1889, for the Exposition universelle of 1889.
- Position: middle, far.
- Silhouette: four legs on a 125 m square base, joined by a big arch; first platform at 57.63 m; legs curve inward to the second platform at 115.73 m; a single slim shaft to the third platform at 276.13 m; small cap; antenna. The outline is a concave curve on each side.
- Local cue: the lattice tower. Cut the lattice as lace holes in the paper.

### Dôme des Invalides
- Height: 107 m at the top (the dome rises to 90 m, the lantern adds the rest).
- Completed: 28 August 1706 (keys handed to Louis XIV).
- Position: left, mid-distance.
- Silhouette: square stone base with a columned portico; tall drum with paired columns; high ribbed dome, steeper than a half sphere, in 12 gilded panels; slim gilded lantern; spire and cross.
- Local cue: the gold dome. It was last regilded in 1989.

### Grand Palais
- Height: total not found in sources. The copper quadrigas on the entrances stand at about 40 m.
- Opened: 1 May 1900, for the Exposition universelle of 1900 (built from 1897).
- Position: right, mid-distance.
- Silhouette: long stone colonnade facade; above it, a long glass barrel roof (the nave, close to 240 m long) rising to a low glass dome over the crossing; copper chariot groups (quadrigas) crown the two main entrances.
- Local cue: the green-grey glass roof with flying horses on top.

### Pont Alexandre-III
- Height: pylon height not found in sources. Main span 107.5 m, total length 160 m, deck 45 m wide.
- Built: 1897 to 1900. Inaugurated 1900.
- Position: middle, near, crossing the river.
- Silhouette: one flat steel arch over the water; at each end, two tall square stone pylons; each pylon carries a gilded bronze group (a winged Fame with a horse). Ornate lamp posts along the deck.
- Local cue: the four gold horses on the pylons.

## Filler

Haussmann blocks along the quays: stone facades with long iron balconies on the second and fifth floors and a grey zinc mansard roof with dormers and chimney pots. Colours are cream limestone and blue-grey zinc. Trees line every quay. The Palais Bourbon is a low classical block with a temple front.
